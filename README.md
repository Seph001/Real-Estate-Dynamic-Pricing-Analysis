# Dynamic Real Estate Pricing Dashboard

## Overview
This project is a real estate analytics and dynamic pricing solution developed in Power BI to monitor property pricing performance, evaluate market demand, analyze price elasticity, and assess the relationship between listed prices, selling prices, and revenue outcomes.

The dashboard transforms real estate and pricing data into actionable insights that support data-driven pricing decisions, revenue optimization, and market performance analysis across multiple cities and property types.

The analysis covers pricing trends, demand sensitivity, market performance, property categories, city-level price behavior, and revenue outcomes from February 2020 to May 2024.

---

# Business Problem
Real estate stakeholders often face challenges such as:

- Inconsistent property pricing
- Poor visibility into price gaps between listed and selling prices
- Limited understanding of market demand sensitivity
- Difficulty optimizing prices across cities and property types
- Revenue leakage from ineffective pricing strategies
- Limited insight into property-level and city-level performance
- Challenges in making timely pricing decisions

This project was designed to provide a centralized real estate intelligence dashboard that enables better pricing, forecasting, planning, and revenue optimization.

# Dashboard Features

## Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of real estate performance including:

- Total Revenue
- Total Units Sold
- Average Listed Price
- Average Selling Price
- Price Gap

---

## Pricing Performance Analysis
Analyzes pricing trends and revenue impact through:

- Avg Listed Price vs Avg Selling Price trends
- Monthly price movement
- Price gap by property type
- Revenue performance by city
- Revenue growth trends

The dashboard identifies:
- Periods of strong selling performance
- Properties with the highest price gap
- Cities contributing the most revenue
- Pricing patterns across the year

---

## Market Demand Intelligence
Provides visibility into demand behavior including:

- Demand Index trends
- Demand reaction to price changes
- Price elasticity by city
- Price elasticity by property type
- Relationship between price and demand

The analysis highlights:
- Market sensitivity to price changes
- Cities with stronger demand response
- Property types with high or low elasticity
- Demand-driven pricing opportunities

---

## Decision Support Engine
Evaluates recommended pricing actions through:

- Suggested price vs listed price
- Price adjustment percentage
- Elasticity score
- Property-level pricing recommendations
- City-level pricing adjustments

This helps stakeholders:
- Optimize property pricing
- Adjust prices based on demand signals
- Improve revenue generation
- Balance competitiveness with profitability

# Key Insights

## Pricing Insights
- Total revenue exceeded 713.4B
- Total units sold reached 10,273
- Average listed price was 66,476,527.18
- Average selling price was 69,452,802.42
- Overall price gap stood at 2,976,275.24

## Demand Insights
- Demand Index averaged 102.73
- 1 Bedroom properties showed strong demand performance
- 2 Bedroom properties showed lower price elasticity in some markets
- Demand patterns varied across cities and property types

## Regional Insights
- Abuja recorded the highest price gap among the tracked cities
- Lagos and Port Harcourt also showed strong revenue performance
- City-level pricing behavior differed significantly
- Regional demand and elasticity influenced pricing recommendations
